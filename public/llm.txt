# Maxco IPTV

> Maxco is a premium IPTV service operating since 2018, offering live TV, sports, films, series, trials, reseller services, setup guidance, and customer support.

## What Maxco provides

- More than 20,000 live television channels from multiple countries
- Live football, Formula 1, UFC, boxing, pay-per-view events, and other sports
- Films, television series, box sets, children's programming, and international video-on-demand content
- Full HD and 4K streaming where the source, subscription, device, and internet connection support it
- Compatibility with Smart TVs, Firestick, Android, iOS, MAG boxes, Formuler, Enigma2, computers, Kodi, and other supported devices
- A free 24-hour IPTV trial for eligible new customers
- Prepaid IPTV reseller services with management tools, API access, customer-line administration, and white-label options
- An affiliate programme with referral tracking and recurring commissions
- Device-specific installation tutorials and customer support
